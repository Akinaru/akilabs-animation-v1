Reference 1 is the logo mark I chose for my slot game studio "Aki Labs": a laboratory flask drawn with a bold white ribbon. The ribbon sweeps down across the flask body and fills the bottom; together with the top it subtly draws a slot-machine "7". I love the thin slit (the narrow black gap that separates the ribbon from the flask's outline) and the 7 idea: keep both.

What I do not like: the cap / rim at the top. It looks like a basic separate rectangle stuck on top, badly integrated. Redesign ONLY the top of the mark (rim + neck) so it belongs to the same drawing as the ribbon: same stroke logic, the rim flowing into the neck and into the top bar of the 7, rounded and refined, like a premium studio logo (benchmark: Paperclip Gaming's mark: simple, bold, drawn with great care).
